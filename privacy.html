Privacy policy (v1.0)

Controller: the event organiser (add name and contact email here before going live).

What we collect

  • The name you type and the photos you upload.
  • The time and version of the policy you consented to.
  • A random identifier in a cookie (vid, 1 year) so we recognise you when you return. We store only a hash of it.

How it is used

Your name and photos are shown publicly on the event display screen. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Legal basis

Your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given by ticking the box before uploading. You can withdraw it at any time.

Storage and retention

Data is kept on the organiser's server and deleted within 30 days after the event (organiser to confirm).

Your rights

Use "Delete all my photos and data" on the upload page to erase everything linked to your device instantly. You may also request access, correction or deletion by emailing the controller, and complain to your data-protection authority.

Do not upload

Photos of other people without their permission, or images of children you are not responsible for.
